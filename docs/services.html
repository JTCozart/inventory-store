Always free

The app is free, and it stays that way

Inventory Store is open source. You can download it, install it on a single PC on your network, and use every feature without any license fee or subscription. That is not a trial, and it is not going away.

When you self-host, your data lives on your own machine. There are no cloud accounts to create, nothing is billed, and you are never locked in. For many teams, that is all they will ever need, and that is exactly how it should be.

Hosted option

Prefer not to run a server? We can host it for you

Running your own machine, keeping it online, setting up a domain, and handling backups is not for everyone. If you would rather skip all of that, we offer a managed hosted option where we run Inventory Store for you and you reach it at your own custom domain.

You get the same app you already know, with the day to day server work handled on your behalf. To keep things simple while the program is small, pricing and details are shared one to one, so reach out and we will walk you through it.

Reach out for hosted details

This is a pilot program, and spots are limited

Hosting is brand new and we are taking on a small number of teams to start, so we can give each one real attention and get the setup right. Early teams help shape where the hosted service goes next. Once the pilot spots are filled, new requests join a waitlist.

Good to know

Common questions

Is the free version really free?

Yes. The downloadable app is free and open source, with every feature included. Hosting is the only paid, optional service.

What does hosting include?

We run Inventory Store for you on your own custom domain and take care of the server, updates, HTTPS, and routine backups, so you can just use the app.

Why are spots limited?

The hosted option is a pilot. We are starting with a small number of teams so we can set each one up carefully and learn what matters most before opening it more widely.

Can I move between the two?

Yes. You can start self-hosted and ask about hosting later, or move your data back to self-hosting at any time. You are never locked in.
