Good to know

Common questions

What does hosting include?

We run Inventory Store for you on your own custom domain and take care of the server, updates, security, and routine backups, so you can just use the app from any device.

What does hosting cost?

We reserve server resources for you on a yearly basis, so hosting is a small annual fee rather than something you pay month to month. We keep pricing personal while the program is small, so reach out and we will talk through what your team needs and share the number.

What is the professional setup option?

For a one-time fee, we get Inventory Store installed and running in your own environment and help your team get started. After the handoff you run it yourself, so this option does not include continued support.

Why are spots limited?

The professional setup and hosted options are both pilots. We are starting with a small number of teams so we can set each one up carefully and learn what matters most before opening it more widely.

Can I move between the options?

Yes. You can start self-hosted and ask about setup help or hosting whenever you are ready. Hosting runs on an annual term, and at the end of the term you can renew or take your data back to self-hosting. Your data is always yours.

Is the free version still free?

Yes. The downloadable app stays free and open source, with every feature included. Hosting and professional setup are the optional paid services for teams who would rather have us handle it.
